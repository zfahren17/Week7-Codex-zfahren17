## Date of Codex Session:

## Analytical Question:  
I recently just purchased a home and having a deck was a big consideration for my wife.  I thought it would be interesting to explore this data set to see (if we control for some obvious factors like square footage, year built, etc...) how that generally influences sales price.

## Summary of Findings:

Across the 776 matched pairs, homes with a wood deck had an average sale price about $4,867 higher than similar homes without one; the median difference was $5,058. 
The decked home sold for more in 57% of pairs, so the overall relationship is modest and not consistent in every neighborhood.
My analysis does appear to show some meaningful variations by neighborhood.  Somerst, SawyerW, and Sawyer had largest differences in sale prices for similar homes so building decks in these areas may be wise investments.
